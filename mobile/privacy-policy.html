In plain English

Location is read only at the moment you tap check-in or check-out, and never in the background.

Camera is used only to scan the check-in QR code — no photo or video is ever saved.

Card details go straight to Stripe — TimeTap's servers never see or store them.

Attendance and pay data belong to your employer's TimeTap organization, not to us.

Nothing is sold. We don't run ads and we don't sell data to anyone.

Deleting your account is one tap in the app, under Profile → Delete account.

01What we collect

02Location

TimeTap uses your device's location for exactly one purpose: to confirm you're at (or near) your workplace when you check in or check out. The app requests "When In Use" permission only — location is read once, at the moment of that action, and is never tracked continuously or collected while the app is in the background or closed.

If your employer has set up a geofence for your branch, your coordinates at check-in are compared against that radius; otherwise they're simply recorded alongside the attendance entry for your employer's records.

03Camera

The camera is used only to scan the rotating QR code displayed at your workplace for check-in/check-out. The camera feed is processed on-device to read the code; no photo, video, or camera frame is ever saved, uploaded, or stored — by TimeTap or by your employer.

04Payments

All card and bank account details are collected and stored directly by Stripe, our payment processor — they pass through TimeTap's servers only as tokenized references, never as raw card or account numbers. Employers subscribe to TimeTap via Stripe Checkout; employees who opt in to digital payouts complete identity verification directly with Stripe (Stripe Connect) to receive funds.

05How your data is used

  • To record attendance, calculate hours worked, and run payroll for your employer.
  • To let your manager review leave requests, overtime/shortfall claims, and cash-out requests.
  • To send you notifications (push and in-app) about things that need your attention — a roster change, an approval, a payment.
  • To keep the service secure and diagnose technical problems.

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not build behavioral profiles for any purpose beyond the attendance/payroll features described here.

06Who we share it with

Attendance, roster, and payroll data is visible to your own employer's managers and moderators — that's the core function of the product. Beyond your employer, we share data only with the service providers that make TimeTap work, each bound to use it solely to provide their service to us:

  • Stripe — payment processing, subscription billing, and payouts.
  • Expo — delivery of push notifications to your device.
  • Our hosting and database providers, who store the data on our behalf.

We never sell personal data, and we never share it with data brokers or advertisers.

07How long we keep it

Attendance and payroll records are retained for as long as your employer's TimeTap account is active, and for a period afterward as required for payroll, tax, and accounting record-keeping. If you delete your own account in the app, your login is deactivated immediately, but your historical attendance and wallet records remain on file under your employer's organization — this is standard practice for payroll systems and mirrors how employment records are legally required to be kept regardless of the software used.

08Your rights & choices

  • Delete your account any time from Profile → Delete account in the app. This deactivates your login immediately; ask your manager if you need it reversed.
  • Access or correct your account details from your Profile, or by asking your manager for anything you can't edit yourself.
  • Location and camera permissions can be reviewed or revoked at any time in your device's Settings — note that check-in/out won't function without them.
  • Push notifications can be turned off in your device's notification settings; you'll still see updates inside the app.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA — contact us using the details below to exercise them.

09Children's privacy

TimeTap is a workplace tool intended for employees of working age and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16.

10Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) between the app and our servers. Access to attendance and payroll data is scoped to your own employer's organization — no other business using TimeTap can see it. Passwords are stored using industry-standard hashing, and no employee of TimeTap can read your password.

11Changes to this policy

If we make a material change to how we handle personal data, we'll update the effective date above and, where the change is significant, notify you in the app.
